## Bundled assets with documented sources

### `assets/cards/`
The currency cards use the **poker deck** in `assets/cards/dark/` (the
`light/` variant is bundled as an alternative skin):

**Pixel Poker Cards** by Aspecs Gaming — **CC0 1.0**:
<https://aspecsgaming.itch.io/mini-pixel-playing-cards>

### `assets/fonts/`
- **Sancreek** by Vernon Adams — **SIL Open Font License 1.1**:
  <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Sancreek>

Sancreek is the one and only UI font (western dark fantasy theme); it
loads as a scalable font at any size. You can drop another font into
this folder, and the font picker in the settings menu lists every
`.ttf` in the folder.

### `assets/ost/`
The soundtrack comes from **"Demon's Realm | Dark Fantasy Music"** by
maxstack — **CC BY-SA 4.0** (attribution + share-alike; the music itself
stays under that licence):
<https://maxstack.itch.io/demons-realm>

The repository ships OGG conversions of the 12 source WAVs (the WAVs stay
local only). Attribution: "Demon's Realm" by maxstack (CC-BY-SA 4.0).

The Hub cue uses **"Devilish Design"** by Johan Brodd — **GPL 3.0**
(<https://opengameart.org/content/devilish-design>). One spare track is
also bundled: "Dark Loop" by Machine — **CC BY 3.0**
(<https://opengameart.org/content/crypt>).

### `assets/ui/`
The inventory, bank and HUD panels come from itsmars on OpenGameArt —
**CC0 1.0**:

- **RPG Inventory** (inventory/bank panel art):
  <https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-inventory>
- **Health Orb 1.1** (the anima/mana orbs):
  <https://opengameart.org/content/health-orb-11>

### `assets/ui-icons/`
1-bit Pixel Icons by Nikoichu — **CC0 1.0**:
<https://nikoichu.itch.io/pixel-icons>

### `assets/ui-sfx/` and `assets/ingame-sfx/`
Sound effects by **Kenney** — **CC0 1.0** (UI Audio, Interface Sounds, RPG
Audio, Impact Sounds, Footsteps packs). Credit appreciated, not required:
<https://kenney.nl>

### `assets/gui/`
Menus draw a full-screen wood board with two parchment sheets and wooden
widgets — western-poster style:

- **RPG GUI Construction Kit v1.0** (wood buttons, gold frame edges,
  checkboxes, paper/wood tiles) by Lamoot — **CC BY 3.0** (attribution
  required):
  <https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-gui-construction-kit-v10>

The GIMP `.xcf` source files are not tracked; the rendered PNGs ship with
the repository. Buttons/panels without the kit textures fall back to
plain raylib-drawn widgets (`ui/gui_kit.py`).

### Combat VFX

Slash, sparkle, flame and glow effects are **generated by code** — GLSL
shaders in `assets/shaders/vfx_*.fs` drawn over a runtime-built white
quad. No VFX art is bundled or required.

### Original assets

These are made in-house, not third-party: `icon.ico`, `assets/logo/`,
`assets/pulse/`, and the item sprites in `assets/items/` (amulets,
armour, helms, rings, scrolls, gems, organs, potions).

### `assets/playable character/knight/`

The knight vessel sprite set is **no longer part of the Lords of Pain
pack**. It is pre-rendered from the **Hero Clint** gunslinger model
(Sketchfab) — **CC BY 4.0** (attribution required):
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

The GLB was rendered with the
[3d-to-isometric](https://github.com/MangasMango/3d-to-isometric) tool
(`convert.py`: 16-direction frames per clip, root motion stripped, Mixamo
junk actions skipped). The rendered PNG frames stay local (gitignored,
like every replaced pack folder), but the underlying model is freely
redistributable under CC BY 4.0, so this character imposes no
distribution restrictions.

Earlier freely licensed knight sets (kept as local backups, not bundled):
the **Armored Knight** by Gabriel Piacenti (**CC BY 3.0**,
<https://opengameart.org/content/knight-2>) in
`knight_piacenti_backup/`, and a Mixamo-rigged paladin (Adobe Mixamo
terms: usable in projects, source files may **not** be redistributed —
rendered sprites only) in `knight_paladin_backup/`.

### `assets/enemy/skeleton/`

The skeleton enemy sprite set is pre-rendered from the **skeleton
character** of the [Flare game](https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game)
project — **CC BY-SA 3.0** (attribution required, see its `CREDITS.txt`):
<https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game>

Flare ships the rigged 3D source (`.blend`); the same 3d-to-isometric
pipeline converted it and re-rendered its original skinning/animation
conventions into our 16-direction frame format, so no engine changes were
needed. Flare-derived assets stay redistributable under CC BY-SA 3.0.

## Assets NOT distributed with this repository

The following folders are **excluded on purpose**. Be aware that **the core
game relies heavily on this pack**: the remaining characters (warrior,
fighter), enemies, bosses and props come from the
**Lords of Pain** pack by trevor-pupkin: <https://trevor-pupkin.itch.io/lords-of-pain>.
The playable **knight** has already been replaced by a freely licensed
model (see the section above); its folder still sits under the same
gitignore umbrella until the whole category is pack-free. The game's UI no
longer needs the pack: the cursor is the system default, ground highlights
and click markers are drawn by code, loot labels are text, and the CRT
vignette lives in `assets/gui/`.
Its licence states:

> LICENCE: This asset pack can be used in both free and commercial projects.
> You can modify it to suit your own needs. **You may not redistribute or
> resell it.**
> <https://trevor-pupkin.itch.io/lords-of-pain>

Excluded folders (each contains a README placeholder; contents are
gitignored):

- `assets/boss/`
- `assets/environment/`
- `assets/enemy/`
- `assets/none-playable character/`
- `assets/playable character/`
- `assets/prop/`

## Getting the assets

To run the game you must obtain the excluded files yourself and copy them into
the matching folders. Everything the game uses is collected here:

[https://itch.io/c/8322662/boundtorot](https://itch.io/c/8322662/boundtorot)

If anything is missing, or you have questions about a licence, contact me on
[Discord](https://discord.com/invite/s2rgHuctdc) or by email at
[mangasmangostudios@gmail.com](mailto:mangasmangostudios@gmail.com).

## Contributing new assets

AI-generated assets are prohibited. Do not submit them; make new art yourself.
For isometric sprites, use my
[3d-to-isometric](https://github.com/MangasMango/3d-to-isometric) tool to make
the process much faster.
